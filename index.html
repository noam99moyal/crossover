    How to play
    • When you have the ball, play a shot face up. The other player can answer with a card that stops it, or let it go.
    • Open shot: Drive and Mid-range score 2. A Three scores 3.
    • Stopped: no points. A stopped Three is a long rebound, so they score 2 on the fast break.
    • Block stops a Drive. Contest stops a Mid-range. Close Out stops a Three.
    • After every shot, both players draw back up to 5 cards (6 if you're behind), and the ball goes to the other player, scored or not.
    • No shot in your hand? You get a new hand.
    • You start with the ball and the CPU starts with 1 point. First to 21 wins.

    Special cards

    Gold shots can only be stopped by a Charge. The other specials are white. A play is never longer than three cards: shot, answer, Crossover.

    Slam Dunk 2 in the deck
    A gold shot worth 2. Only a Charge stops it.
    Buzzer Beater 1 in the deck
    A gold shot worth 3. Only a Charge stops it. It's not a Three, so stopping it gives no fast break.
    Crossover offense, 2 in the deck
    After they answer your shot, their card misses and your shot scores. It beats any answer.
    Charge defense, 2 in the deck
    Stops any shot, even gold.
    Steal defense, 2 in the deck
    Stops a Drive or Mid-range, and you score 2.
    Foul defense, 2 in the deck
    Can't stop it? Their shot scores just 1 point. Works on gold shots too.

    The computer

    It always answers when it can, and it keeps track of which cards are gone before it picks a shot.
