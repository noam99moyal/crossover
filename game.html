    How to play

    • When you have the ball, play a shot card: Drive, Mid-range, Three, Slam Dunk or Buzzer Beater.
    • The other player can stop it with an answer card. Nothing stops it, or want to keep your card? Let it go and it scores.
    • Scores: Drive and Mid-range are worth 2. A Three is worth 3.
    • Stopped: no points. Charge stops a Drive. Contest stops a Mid-range. Close Out stops a Three, and the defense scores 2.
    • After every shot, both players fill their hand back up to 5 cards, 6 if they're behind. Then the ball goes to the other player, scored or not.
    • No shot in your hand? Swap it for a new hand until you have one.
    • You start with the ball. First to 21 wins.
    • Press and hold any card to see it big. Tap anywhere to put it back.

    Special cards

    Specials have gold frames. Slam Dunk and Buzzer Beater are gold shots: only a Block stops them, and a Foul makes them score just 1. A shot never takes more than three cards: the shot, one answer and a Pump Fake.

    The computer

    It always answers when it can, and it keeps track of which cards are gone before it picks a shot.
